Common questions.

Will Parrot spam my chat?
No. Parrot batches messages over a 30-second window and answers everyone with the same question in one reply. After answering, it cools down for several minutes before repeating itself.
Can someone trick Parrot into saying something offensive?
Multi-layer defense. The model treats chat as data, not instructions. Responses go through a content filter. Nothing from chat is posted verbatim. Repeated injection attempts auto-block the user.
What if Parrot doesn't know the answer?
It stays silent. Parrot only answers from your knowledge base — it never guesses. Unanswered questions show up on your end-of-stream report so you can add the gaps.
Does this work on YouTube?
Twitch only for now. YouTube support is coming.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard. No emails, no calls, no friction.
